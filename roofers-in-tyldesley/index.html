Roofing in Tyldesley

Tyldesley climbs a hillside, with the old cotton-town streets around Top o' th' Town looking out across the mosslands towards Astley, where the colliery headgear still stands at Astley Green. The older housing is terraced, much of it under slate, with inter-war council houses and later estates filling in around it, and a lot of new building towards Astley and Mosley Common since the guided busway opened. A roof on a hillside takes the weather harder than one in a hollow, so wind-lifted slates and ridge tiles, and flashings pulled away from chimneys, are common here. The newer estates mostly need gutter, fascia and flat-roof work rather than anything structural.
