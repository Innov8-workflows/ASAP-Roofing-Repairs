A family business

ASAP Roofing is a family business. Rob's father started it, and when he retired Rob took it on. It is still family run today, with a team of five.

We work for homeowners, landlords and letting agencies right across the North West, with most of our work in Wigan, Leigh and Manchester. We do new roofs and re-roofing, roof repairs, flat roofing, chimney work, and fascias, soffits and guttering, and we run a 24-hour emergency call-out for leaks and storm damage.
