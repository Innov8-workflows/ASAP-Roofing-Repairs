Roofing in Worsley

Worsley's village, with the Bridgewater Canal running orange from the old mine workings and its black-and-white estate buildings, is a conservation area, so work on roofs there needs to respect what is already there. Beyond the village the housing is mostly larger detached and semi-detached homes from the 1930s onwards, with Boothstown, Ellenbrook and Roe Green filled with estates from the 1970s to the present day. Bigger houses mean bigger roofs with more hips, valleys and dormers, so valley and lead work, ridge repairs and re-roofs on the older homes are common, along with fascias, soffits and gutters on the estates.
