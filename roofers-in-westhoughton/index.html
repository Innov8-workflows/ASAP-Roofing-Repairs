Roofing in Westhoughton

Westhoughton sits on the ridge between Bolton and Wigan, and while its centre has the terraces and chapels of an old weaving and mining town, much of it is estates built from the 1960s onwards around Daisy Hill, Wingates and Hart Common, with plenty of newer homes since. Being on higher ground, roofs here catch the wind, and loose ridges, lifted tiles and cracked verges are common after a stormy winter. The older terraces bring slate repairs and chimney work, and the estates mostly need fascias, soffits and guttering replaced, flat roofs on extensions renewed, and in time a full re-roof where the original felt has given out.
